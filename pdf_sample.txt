1. Scope — Covers the manufacture and chemical and
physical  requirements of 33 grade ordinary Portland
cement.
2. Chemical Requirements — When tested in
accordance with the methods given in IS 4032 : 1985, 33
grade ordinary Portland cement shall comply with the
chemical requirements given in Table 1.
3. Physical Requirements
3.1 Fineness — Specific surface of cement shall not be
less than 225 m2 /kg.
3.2 Soundness — Unaerated expansion shall be not more

autoclave test; if it fails, aerated sample shall not show
more of than 5 mm and 0.6 percent when tested by 'Le
Chatelier' method and autoclave method respectively.
